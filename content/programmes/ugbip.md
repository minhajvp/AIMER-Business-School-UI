\## Internships: two 6-month industry experiences



Internships form a critical part of the UGBIP curriculum, designed to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world business environments. Through two structured internship phases, each lasting six months, students gain hands-on exposure to corporate and startup ecosystems while applying academic concepts in practical settings.



The objective of these internships is to develop professional confidence, industry awareness, and role-specific skills while preparing students for real workplace challenges. By the end of the program, students graduate with a full year of real industry experience, enhanced employability, and a clear understanding of how businesses operate across functions.
