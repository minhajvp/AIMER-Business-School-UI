\### Eligibility criteria for PGBIP



To apply for the PGBIP program, applicants must meet the following eligibility



\### Educational Qualification



A minimum 3-year \*\*Bachelor's degree\*\* from a recognized university or an equivalent professional qualification in



\*\*ACCA:\*\* All 13 papers completed + membership requirements fulfilled.



\*\*ICMAI:\*\* Final Level completed + membership attained.



\### Language Proficiency



Good Knowledge of \*\*English\*\* (reading, writing, and speaking) is essential.



\### Entrance Requirements



Successful performance in the \*\*Interview\*\* and \*\*National AIMER Admission Test (NAAT).\*\*



\### Passion \& Interest



A demonstrated interest in \*\*business management, entrepreneurship, or leadership.\*\*
