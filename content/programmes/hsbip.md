\## Value delivery architecture: 11 pillars



\### Core Academic Syllabus



Standard Plus One \& Plus Two Commerce curriculum governed by SCOLE Kerala.



\### Additional Business Modules



Supplementary coursework focused on practical business dynamics \& venture creation.



\### Student Development Programs (SDP)



Soft skills, communication, emotional intelligence, and leadership workshops.



\### Technical Bootcamps



Hands-on, hard-skills training (no-code, financial modeling, marketing tools, MS Office).



\### Experiential Expeditions



Business Exploration to Gain Real-World Experience.



\### Guest Talk Series



Interactions with active startup founders, corporate Leaders, and industry veterans.



\### Outbound Training (OBT)



Off-campus leadership camps, team-building retreats, and survival/resilience challenges.



\### Junior Venture Studio



In-house incubator enabling students to build real Tech \& Non-Tech Startups.



\### Social Impact



Community-driven activities and social responsibility initiatives.



\### Competitions \& Hackathons



Participation in internal and external pitch deck presentations, declamations, and hackathons.



\### Management Challenges



Real-world problem-solving sprints and simulated business crisis scenarios.



Collectively, these 11 pillars turn a standard commerce curriculum into a complete entrepreneurship ecosystem.
