## Questions from companies

What programs does AIMER offer, and how do they benefit companies?

AIMER offers undergraduate and postgraduate work-integrated programs with a strong emphasis on experiential learning, leadership development, and business innovation.

How can companies hire AIMER students for internships and full-time roles?

Companies can partner with AIMER through our Campus Recruitment Program. Contact the Corporate Relations team at corporate@aimerbschool.com to participate in the hiring process.

What kind of internship opportunities can companies offer?

Internships where students contribute to real business challenges while gaining hands-on experience, across marketing, HR, finance, operations and entrepreneurship.
