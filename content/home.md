Got Questions?



\## Frequently Asked Questions.



Straight answers to common questions about admissions, internships, safety, and degrees.





How does AIMER's practice-led model differ from a traditional B-school?



Traditional business schools focus primarily on classroom lectures and memorization. AIMER provides 75% experiential learning, where students work up to 12 months inside real companies, solve live business briefs, build startups in the on-campus incubation hub, and complete international business delegations in Dubai and Singapore.



What is the National AIMER Aptitude Test (NAAT) and who needs to take it?



NAAT is the comprehensive national entrance and merit scholarship evaluation test for admission into HSBIP (+1 and +2), UGBIP (Undergraduate), and PGBIP (Postgraduate) programmes. High performers can qualify for up to 100% merit tuition scholarships.



Are the corporate internships paid, and what stipends do students receive?



Yes, corporate internships at AIMER partner companies are paid. Students earn competitive monthly stipends ranging up to ₹50,000 per month depending on their performance and corporate role.



What are the residential hostel, dining, and safety arrangements at Markaz Knowledge City?



AIMER is situated inside the 125-acre gated Markaz Knowledge City township. It features 24/7 security, biometric access, separate residential hostels for male and female students with resident faculty wardens, hygienic multi-cuisine dining, and strict anti-ragging policies. \[Read our Parent Guide →](https://www.aimerbschool.com/parents/)



Can students start their own business on campus?



Yes, every student has access to the AIMER Venture Launchpad. Multiple student startups, including ZYNO, Zordar, and She Kit, were founded and incubated by our students on campus, generating real revenue before graduation.



What fee payment options, bank loans, and EMI plans are available?



AIMER offers flexible 0% interest institutional monthly EMI plans and comprehensive documentation support for educational loans through nationalized and private commercial banks.



Are the degrees recognized by universities and UGC?



Yes, all degree qualifications completed through AIMER are fully accredited and conferred in affiliation with recognized state and central universities in compliance with UGC and NEP 2020 guidelines.
