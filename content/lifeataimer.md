## Student living & hostel

Everything a residential student needs, on or next to campus.

## Student Representative Council

The Student Representative Council (SRC) connects students with faculty and administration, enhancing the AIMER experience through student-led events. Elected representatives ensure the SRC reflects the student community's voice.
